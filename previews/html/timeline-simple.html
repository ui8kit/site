How a project runs

Four steps from the first call to the launch, usually in a month.

  1. Day 1

    Discovery call

    One hour on your goals, your audience and the pages you need.

  2. Week 1

    Prototype

    Wireframes from ready blocks, so you see the whole site early.

  3. Weeks 2 to 3

    Build and content

    Real texts and pictures in the blocks, tested on phones and desktops.

  4. Week 4

    Launch

    The domain, analytics and a short training for your team.
