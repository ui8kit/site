    Campaigns: Notices

    6 sections between the top bar and the footer of the template.

    Install

    The blocks of this page, top bar and footer included:

    npx ui8kit add nav-bar page-intro announcement-bar cookie-banner newsletter-form footer-split

    Blocks: nav-bar, page-intro, announcement-bar, cookie-banner, newsletter-form, footer-split
