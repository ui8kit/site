    Pricing pages: Plans

    5 sections between the top bar and the footer of the template.

    Install

    The blocks of this page, top bar and footer included:

    npx ui8kit add nav-bar page-intro pricing-tiers pricing-card comparison-table sitemap-footer

    Blocks: nav-bar, page-intro, pricing-tiers, pricing-card, comparison-table, sitemap-footer
