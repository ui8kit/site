    Templates / Product pages

    Product pages: Tour

    6 sections between the top bar and the footer of the template.

    Install

    The blocks of this page, top bar and footer included:

    npx ui8kit add nav-bar page-intro feature-sticky before-after steps-list video-grid mega-menu sitemap-footer

    Blocks: nav-bar, page-intro, feature-sticky, before-after, steps-list, video-grid, mega-menu, sitemap-footer

    Preview

    The sections of the page; the top bar and the footer have their own block pages.

    Take the tour

    Four stops: the list, the steps, the film and the menu.

    See the capabilities

    Follow the story

    Fast by default

    Pages render from static data and ship little script.

    Clear structure

    One heading per page, landmarks and a skip link come built in.

    Safe forms

    Inputs carry labels and errors are announced to assistive tools.

    Ready for any language

    Every text lives in a dictionary, so a new language is one more file.

    npx ui8kit init --yes --framework svelte --dir src --registry-url https://registry.buildy.tw/svelte --strict-cdn
