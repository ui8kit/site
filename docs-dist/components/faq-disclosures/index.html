    Faq Disclosures

    Faq Disclosures component

    Install

    npx ui8kit add faq-disclosures

    Pulls in: disclosure, faq-split, stack, text, utils

    Preview

    How do I start a new site?

    Pick a template, change the texts in one dictionary and publish. Most sites are live in an hour.

    Can I use my own domain?

    Yes. Point your domain at the site and we issue the certificate for you.

    When am I billed?

    On the same day each month. You can switch to yearly billing and save two months.

    Do you give refunds?

    Within 30 days of a payment, no questions asked.

    How do I invite my team?

    Open the settings, add their addresses and choose what each person can edit.

    Can I take my content with me?

    Every plan exports all pages and texts as plain files at any time.
