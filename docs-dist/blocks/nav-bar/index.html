    Nav Bar

    A top bar in four shapes: inline, split around the brand, with a search field, and transparent over a picture.

    Install

    npx ui8kit add nav-bar

    Pulls in: block, box, container, grid, group, link, link-plain, nav-actions, nav-block, nav-links, over-media, search-palette, utils

    A top bar in four shapes. Axes: layout (inline: brand left, links, button right; split: the brand in the middle with the links on both sides; search: a search field with its shortcut in the middle), over (none, media: the bar sits over a picture, transparent), actions mode (none, cta), tone (default, inverse: on a dark hero band). Turning solid on scroll is a port contract.
